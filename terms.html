2. Booking requests

Sending the booking form or an email is a request, not a confirmed booking. Your session is only confirmed once I've replied to confirm the date, time, location, session length and price, and you've completed any payment I ask for in that confirmation. 3. Prices and payment

Prices on the pricing page are in Australian dollars and are the full price of the session for the length listed. Travel outside the suburbs listed on the pricing page may cost extra, and I'll tell you before you book. Extra hours on the day are charged at the hourly rate on the pricing page, and only with your agreement. Payment amounts, deposits and due dates are set out in your booking confirmation.

4. Changing or cancelling a session

If you need to reschedule, please tell me as early as you can and I'll do my best to find another date. Any cancellation or rescheduling fees, and whether a deposit is refundable, are set out in your booking confirmation.

If I'm unable to photograph your session because of illness, an emergency or circumstances outside my control, I'll offer you another date, try to arrange a suitable replacement photographer, or refund what you've paid for the session. For outdoor sessions, if the weather makes photography unsafe or impractical, we'll agree on a new date together.

  • I'll always put the safety of you, your family and your baby first. For newborn sessions, I won't attempt any pose that I don't consider safe.
  • I may stop or pause a session if I feel unsafe or am treated disrespectfully.

I take as many photos as the session allows, then choose and edit the best of them in my own style. There's no set number of photos, and I don't provide unedited or raw files. I'll deliver your edited photos as an online gallery within the time shown on the pricing page for your session. Those times are a guide, and I'll let you know if I expect a delay.

Please download and back up your photos when you receive them. I keep a backup for a reasonable time, but I can't guarantee to keep it forever.
