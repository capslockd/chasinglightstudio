Privacy Policy

The short version: I only collect what I need to arrange and photograph your session, I never sell it, and you can ask me to see, fix or delete it at any time.

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Chasing Light Studio (“I”, “me”, “my”) handles personal information collected through chasinglightstudios.com.au and when you book a photography session. I follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect

  • Booking requests and enquiries. When you use the booking form on the pricing page or email me, I receive your name, your email address and/or phone number, how you'd like to be contacted, the session and length you're interested in, your preferred date, your suburb or location, and anything else you choose to tell me.
  • Session details. If you go ahead with a booking, I may also keep details needed to plan and deliver it, such as the venue, timings, names of the people being photographed, and payment records.
  • Photographs. The photos I take during your session are personal information about the people in them.
  • Technical information. Like any website, the companies that host and serve this site automatically receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested.

I don't ask for sensitive information. If you choose to share something sensitive with me (for example, health details for a maternity or newborn session), I'll use it only to look after you during the session.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, check my availability and arrange your session.
  • To send you a copy of your booking request, if you gave an email address.
  • To photograph, edit and deliver your photos, and to keep records for tax and accounting.
  • To show selected photos in my portfolio and on social media, only with your permission (see below).

I won't send you marketing emails, and I never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Your photos, my portfolio and social media

I'll ask you before I share photos from your session on this website, Instagram or Facebook. You can say no, choose which photos are shared, or ask for a gallery to be password protected. If you change your mind later, email me and I'll remove the photos from this website and my own social accounts as soon as I reasonably can. I can't control copies that other people have already saved or reshared.

If you're photographed as a guest at someone else's event and would rather not appear in my portfolio, just let me know.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies, and it doesn't track you across other websites. When you unlock a password-protected gallery, your browser remembers that until you close the tab (using “session storage”), so you don't have to re-enter the password on every page. That information never leaves your device.

Who I share it with

I use a small number of trusted services to run the website and my business. They only receive what they need to do their job:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google stores booking requests in a Google Sheet, and serves the fonts used on this site (Google Fonts).
  • Brevo sends booking notification and confirmation emails.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain and forwards email sent to my studio address.
  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook) hosts any photos I share there with your permission.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union. I may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep booking requests that don't go ahead for up to 12 months, then delete them. For sessions I photograph, I keep your contact and booking details for as long as I need to for tax and legal reasons (generally five years), and I keep a backup of your photos so I can help if you lose your copy. You can ask me to delete your photos sooner.

Keeping it safe

I store your information in password-protected accounts and devices and take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No method of storing or sending information over the internet is completely secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days. You don't have to give your name to ask a general question, but I'll need to know who you are to make a booking.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a concern about how I've handled your information, please email moments@chasinglightstudios.com.au and I'll do my best to put it right. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
